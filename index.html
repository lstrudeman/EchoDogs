Echo Dogs White Shepherd Rescue

Echo Dogs White Shepherd Rescue is a 501(c)3 nonprofit, foster based organization of volunteer professionals dedicated to saving white German shepherd dogs from mistreatment and unnecessary death simply because they are unwanted and unloved. We provide them with a safe, secure foster home, where they receive necessary veterinary care and behavioral training and match them with families that are committed to providing a safe, loving home environment for their canine family member.

We operate east of the Mississippi.

Seventeen years of intake and outcome

Rescues and adoptions track each other closely — a dog pulled in December is often adopted in the new year, which is why the two series cross rather than sit on top of each other.

Rescued and adopted, by year

The programme roughly doubled in 2018. Intake held steady through the first pandemic year, then hit its all-time peak in 2021 — 252 dogs in, 239 out — and has eased back since.
